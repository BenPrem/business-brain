# Social Media Video Production Standards

**Scope:** every reel, short, or vertical video ad produced for any client.
**Source basis:** algorithm research across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok (2025–2026 data) plus agency workflow analysis; sources listed at the bottom. **Re-research quarterly — algorithms change fast, and every figure below is dated to this research window.**

---

## 1. The 3-Second Rule (non-negotiable)

The research window's numbers: up to 50% of viewers drop off inside 3 seconds; videos holding 60%+ of viewers at the 3-second mark outperform weak hooks by 5–10x in reach; roughly 72% of viral reels open with a storytelling hook or jump cut inside 3 seconds.

Every reel must open with:
- A visual hook in the first 1 second — motion, a face in frame, a pattern interrupt, or a bold visual change.
- A text overlay visible within the first 1 second (one second, not 1.5).
- Audio from frame 1 — music or voice, never a slow fade-in.

Hook formats that work (pick one per reel):
- Question hook aimed at the viewer's real situation
- Pattern interrupt: unexpected visual, contrarian claim, curiosity gap
- Emotion hook: a subject showing a feeling the viewer recognizes
- Curiosity text: "You're doing this wrong..." / "Wait for it..."
- Face-first: reels opening on a face show ~35% higher retention

Hook formats that kill reach: logo reveals and brand intros, slow fades from black, text that arrives after 1 second, silent openings.

## 2. Length by Platform

| Platform | Optimal length | Why (research window) |
|----------|---------------|------|
| Instagram Reels | 7–30s | best completion + new-audience reach |
| Facebook Reels | 15–30s | ~45% higher completion than longer cuts; 50% retention at halfway ≈ 4x reach |
| TikTok | 7–15s | ~70% completion needed for second-batch FYP promotion |
| YouTube Shorts | 15–60s | more tolerance for longer content |

Default when unsure: 10–15 seconds. Tighter always beats padded — no dead air, no filler, no static hold longer than 2 seconds.

## 3. Audio Strategy

Silent reels are dead; trending audio lifts retention meaningfully (≈40% in the cited research).

**The 60/40 rule:** ~60% of reels on trending audio (algorithmic reach), ~40% on original voiceover or brand audio (authority and voice).

Trending-audio process:
1. Monitor TikTok weekly for rising sounds — TikTok trends typically reach Instagram 3–7 days later.
2. A sound trending on TikTok but under ~5,000 uses on Instagram Reels is the early-mover window.
3. Produce and post within 24–48 hours of spotting a riser.
4. Commercially licensed audio only — copyright strikes kill reach and client trust.
5. Track risers via scheduler-tool weekly roundups (HeyOrca, Buffer, Metricool).

Audio production rules:
- Music starts at frame 1; never fade in.
- Voiceover at 100%; background music 30–40% under voiceover, 60–70% standalone.
- Export two versions of every reel: with embedded audio, and without (so platform-native trending audio can be applied).

## 4. Text Overlays and Captions

Overlay text is a ranking signal, not decoration. From the cited research window: ~18% better performance with overlays, ~40% more saves, ~12% longer watch time, ~42% more engagement when captions carry a clear CTA, ~85% of video watched muted — and Instagram crawls overlay text for search and Explore distribution.

Text rules:
- Overlays on every scene, appearing within 1 second of the scene start (short fade-up, never a static pop).
- Client brand fonts and brand colors only — never editor defaults.
- Subtle shadow or outline for readability over busy backgrounds.
- 1–2 lines per scene, short and scannable.
- CTA in the final scene or caption.

Caption (description) strategy:
- First line repeats or extends the hook.
- Work in 3–5 niche keywords naturally.
- End with a conversation-starting question or CTA — comments boost distribution.
- 5–10 hashtags, mixing broad and niche-specific.

## 5. Transitions and Pacing

Between scenes: quick cuts (0.1–0.15s) or fast wipes; short crossfades (~0.3s) only between very different compositions. Never slow dissolves (0.5s+) — they produce amateur-looking ghosting. Never hold a static frame beyond 2 seconds without motion.

Within scenes: always some motion — a 2–3% zoom across the scene, a slow pan, or parallax. Static reads amateur; motion reads professional.

Pacing template for a 10-second reel: 0–3s hook → 3–7s value/story → 7–10s CTA.

## 6. AI-Generated Content Policy

Platform enforcement is real: research in this window reported Instagram reach penalties of 23–80% on AI-labeled content, and TikTok removing tens of thousands of synthetic-media videos per half-year. Policy positions as of this window: TikTok bans unlabeled AI content from creator monetization; labeled AI content ranks normally.

Acceptable uses:
- AI-assisted motion on real assets (parallax/zoom/pan over actual illustrations or photos)
- AI voiceover for narration when the client can't record
- AI for concepting, storyboards, and internal drafts
- AI test clips that inform creative direction

Risky for public posting:
- Fully AI-generated animation as the primary content
- AI scenes with no real creative assets as input
- Anything that *looks* AI-generated: uncanny motion, characters inconsistent between shots

**The rule: AI is a production tool, not a replacement for creative assets.** Lead with real illustrations, footage, and voice; use AI to enhance. For clients with professional illustration or photography, AI motion on top of real art is the sweet spot — animated feel without the AI-content penalty.

## 7. Production Workflow (batching)

Context switching kills throughput. Batch everything.

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Mon | Script the week's reels (5–7 scripts, 15–20 min each); research trending audio |
| Tue | Produce all reels in one session (4–6 hours for 5) |
| Wed | Client review and revisions |
| Thu–Sun | Scheduled publishing |

Per-reel steps: script (10m) → prep visual assets (10m) → generate any AI motion clips (15m passive) → select audio (5m) → assemble (15–20m) → brand-font text overlays (10m) → export both audio versions (5m) → caption with keywords/hashtags/CTA (5m) → schedule (5m).

Target: under 90 minutes per reel in a batched session once templates exist.

## 8. Posting Cadence and Timing

| Platform | Frequency | Best days | Best times (local) |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Instagram Reels | 4x/week | Tue–Thu, Sat | early afternoon |
| Facebook Reels | daily (5x/week minimum) | Wed, Thu | 9am–3pm |
| TikTok | 3–4x/week | Tue–Thu | early afternoon |
| YouTube Shorts | 2–3x/week | any | any |

Key rule from the research window: Facebook gave a ~50% distribution boost to same-day uploads — upload on publish day, never days in advance.

Cross-posting: adapt per platform (caption length, hashtags, sometimes ratio). Identical cross-posts underperform; each algorithm rewards native-feeling content.

## 9. Content Mix — the 40/30/20/10 Framework

For any client's monthly calendar:

| Category | Share | Looks like |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Evergreen/educational | 40% | tips, how-tos, teaching content that stays relevant |
| Trending/seasonal | 30% | trending-audio reels, seasonal hooks, timely moments |
| Community/UGC | 20% | reposting customer content, challenges, behind-the-scenes, comment engagement |
| Promotional | 10% | direct product/service promotion, launches, offers |

UGC priority: user-generated content drove ~65% more engagement than brand content in the cited research. Building a UGC flywheel — encouraging customers to share, featuring them, running community challenges — is among the highest-ROI activities for consumer brands. Include a soft share CTA in most reels ("tag someone who...", "send this to..."): DM shares were Instagram's top-weighted ranking signal in this window.

## 10. Platform Algorithm Cheat Sheet (as of this research window)

**Instagram Reels:** top signals watch time, likes-per-reach, DM shares. Average reel reach rate ~31% vs ~14% carousels / ~13% static. 60%+ 3-second hold ≈ 5–10x reach. Overlay text is crawled for search/Explore. AI-labeled content penalized.

**Facebook Reels:** 50% retention at the halfway mark ≈ 4x reach; ~50% same-day upload bonus; audience skews older; relevance surveys weight niche clarity over raw engagement; all video is Reels now.

**TikTok:** completion threshold for second-batch FYP promotion ~70% (raised from 50%); watch time and search relevance are the heaviest signals; ~71% of viewers decide in the first 1–2 seconds; unlabeled AI content excluded from creator monetization.

## 11. Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Hook lands within 1 second (text visible, motion present, audio playing)
- [ ] Text overlays on every scene, brand font and color
- [ ] Audio starts at frame 1
- [ ] No static hold beyond 2 seconds
- [ ] Clean transitions — no ghosting dissolves
- [ ] 1080x1920 minimum resolution
- [ ] All text/faces/logos/CTAs inside the platform safe zone (`guides/social-media-safe-zones.md`)
- [ ] Cross-platform posts fit the universal safe zone (~851x1070px center rectangle)
- [ ] CTA in the final scene AND the caption
- [ ] Caption has 3–5 niche keywords + a conversation-starting question
- [ ] 5–10 hashtags, broad + specific mix
- [ ] Both export versions exist (with and without audio)
- [ ] Nothing looks obviously AI-generated
- [ ] Brand colors/fonts throughout
- [ ] Under 30 seconds total (ideally 10–15)

## 12. Adapting to a Vertical (worked example: family/parenting brands)

Every vertical has its own winning formats — research them before producing. As one example, for children's-product and family-faith brands the cited research found: behind-the-scenes and creator-story content, short read-aloud or product-in-use clips over real art, parent UGC (kids' reactions), "products for kids who need [specific virtue/skill]" formats, and educator endorsements all outperform; polished brand ads and context-free product reveals underperform. The buyer is usually a parent buying a *transformation* (help raising their child), not a product — content must speak to that. Email integrates tightly in this vertical (children's-content email open rates ran well above retail average in the cited research), and seeding niche micro-influencers (10K–200K followers) before a launch outperformed weeks of organic posting. Extract the same shape of insight for whatever vertical the client is in.
