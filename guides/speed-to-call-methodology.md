# Speed-to-Call Methodology

## The Rule: Service-Business Websites Get Scheduling Questionnaires, Not Contact Forms

For every service-based client (flooring, HVAC, dental, legal, real estate, contracting, and similar), the primary website CTA is a multi-step scheduling questionnaire — never a plain contact form.

## The Standard 3-Step Flow

**Step 1 — Service selection.** Visual cards let the customer pick what they need. This segments the lead instantly.

**Step 2 — Project details.** Industry-specific qualifying questions: scope, timeline, budget range (optional), location, urgency. The lead arrives pre-qualified.

**Step 3 — Book a time.** An interactive calendar with real availability. The customer picks a slot, enters name and phone, and gets instant confirmation.

## Why This Is Non-Negotiable

- Plain contact-form leads show up to follow-up calls at materially lower rates than self-scheduled calls (in agency experience, roughly 30–40% vs 75–85% — treat these as working benchmarks, not published statistics).
- Contact forms produce cold "call me sometime" leads; scheduled questionnaires produce warm, pre-qualified appointments.
- The business gets all project details BEFORE the call, so they show up prepared and the customer feels heard.
- The speed-to-lead research is unambiguous: the classic Harvard Business Review lead-response study ("The Short Life of Online Sales Leads," HBR, 2011) found firms responding within an hour were roughly seven times likelier to qualify the lead than those waiting even an hour longer, and follow-up work on the same dataset showed contact rates collapse within minutes. A self-serve scheduler makes the response effectively instant.

## Implementation

- Use a real scheduling backend — Calendly (free tier) or Cal.com (open source) both work.
- Embed the flow directly on the site as the primary CTA; don't link away to a bare booking page.
- Customize the questionnaire per industry, but always capture: service type, scope/size, timeline, budget (optional), preferred contact method.

## Industry Question Templates

- **Flooring:** type, rooms, square footage, timeline, budget
- **HVAC:** service type (install/repair/maintenance), system age, home size, urgency
- **Dental:** new or existing patient, service needed, insurance, preferred day/time
- **Legal:** case type, brief description, timeline, consultation preference
- **Real estate:** buy/sell/both, area, price range, timeline
- **General contractor:** project type, scope, timeline, budget range

Customize per client, but always keep the pattern: **Select → Detail → Schedule.**
