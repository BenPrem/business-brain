# Email Composer

One-off operational emails to existing relationships: clients, vendors, partners.

## Voice (non-negotiable)

Professional but approachable. **"We" not "I"** in anything client-facing (presents as
a team). No emojis in client-facing email. Lead with the recipient's problem or need,
not your own update. Banned filler (same list as cold-email): "I hope this email finds
you well," "I wanted to reach out," "touch base," "circle back," "synergy,"
"leverage." Sign off with <YOUR NAME> or "<YOUR NAME> — <YOUR BUSINESS>." Never
"Best regards."

## Before drafting

Read the recipient's record first — the contact/client record in your workspace and
recent activity notes for relationship state, open commitments, and the last
touchpoint. Only ask the founder for context the records do not hold. Never state a
fact about the relationship (dates, promises, amounts) you cannot see in a record —
guessed facts in a real email are how trust dies.

If any client has industry-specific compliance rules on file (financing language,
regulated claims, legal disclaimers), check the email against them before it goes
anywhere.

## Scenario shapes (not scripts)

**Client status update.** Subject is concrete: "<CLIENT> site — this week's progress."
Body: (1) what shipped, plain-language bullets; (2) what is next plus any date that
moved; (3) one clear ask, or "nothing needed from you." Flag risks early — never bury
a slipped date.

**Vendor / partner request.** Subject names the deliverable: "<CLIENT> transfers —
domain + business-profile status." Body: one line of context, the requested items as a
checklist, a date ("does Friday work?"), and what it unblocks. Firm, courteous, zero
filler. Cite prior commitments from the activity log, not memory.

**Graceful decline.** Thank them and name the specific thing declined; one honest
sentence of reason (capacity, focus); leave a door open only if the founder actually
wants one. Never commit future work as a softener.

## Output

Subject + body, plus a 1-2 sentence note flagging anything that needs the founder's
judgment (tone risk, implied commitments). The founder sends — the agent never sends
directly.

## After the founder sends

1. Log an activity note in the workspace record: date, `email-sent`, a 1-2 sentence
   summary, any follow-up date. Verify the file exists on disk.
2. Update the matching task in <TASK SYSTEM> (comment + due date if it moved).
